Badge — a small status pill. Always pair color with a word (✓ উপস্থিত, not a bare green dot) — brief §23 bans color-only status. `accent` (purple) is reserved for exam/assignment tags; don't reach for it as a sixth free color.

```jsx
<Badge tone="success">✓ উপস্থিত</Badge>
<Badge tone="danger">✕ অনুপস্থিত</Badge>
```
