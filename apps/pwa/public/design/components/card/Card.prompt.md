Card — a surface for grouping related information (today's classes, a stat, a notice). Use it only when grouping genuinely helps; don't wrap every row of information in its own card (brief §26). One shadow tone (`--shadow-sm`), one radius (`--radius-lg`), no colored border accent.

```jsx
<Card kicker="আজকের ক্লাস" title="৮ম শ্রেণি — গণিত" meta="৮:০০ AM" />
```
