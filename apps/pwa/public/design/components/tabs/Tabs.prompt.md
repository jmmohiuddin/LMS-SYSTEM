Tabs — underline style, used to split a profile or a screen into a few groups (Overview | Attendance | Results | Fees on a student profile, brief §11). Keep to 2–4 tabs; more belongs in নমো navigation, not tabs.

<Tabs items={[{value:'overview',label:'ওভারভিউ'},{value:'attendance',label:'উপস্থিতি'}]} active="overview" />
